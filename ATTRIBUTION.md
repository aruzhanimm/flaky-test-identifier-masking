# Source attribution and terms

The canonical diagnostic observations derive from **ReproFlake Results**, Zenodo, [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18451345](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18451345). The archived source record declares **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**. The associated source snapshots are available at [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18634501](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18634501), whose retained record also declares CC BY 4.0. Original metadata records are retained in `data/source` and `evidence/extension_v5_data/public`.

ReproFlake is described by S. Rafi and colleagues in *A Dataset of Reproducible Flaky-Test Failures*, [DOI 10.1145/3832216](https://doi.org/10.1145/3832216). Test configurations and labels retain their source attribution, including ReproFlake's iDoFT/JIRA origins. Repository URLs, revisions and archived member identifiers are preserved with each observation.

This analysis selects confirmed exact-target failures, normalises diagnostic text and produces paired identifier-masked text. These transformations are documented in the protocols and executable masking code. The source data do not endorse the analysis or its conclusions.

Retained Java files and excerpts are provenance evidence from their respective repositories, including Elide and WildFly Core. Their copyright notices and license headers are preserved. Follow the upstream repository licenses for third-party source code; a dataset license is not presented as overriding those terms. Public issue/commit metadata retains its upstream links.

No blanket relicensing is applied to this collection of analysis code, diagnostic data and third-party source artifacts. For reuse, cite the analysis release and the original sources and comply with the terms applicable to the material being reused.
